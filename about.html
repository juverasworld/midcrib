About Us – MIDCRIB LIMITED

Who We Are

MIDCRIB LIMITED is a premier digital solutions provider headquartered in Nigeria, serving clients across Africa and beyond. Since our establishment in 2017, we have consistently delivered innovative, reliable, and scalable IT solutions that help individuals, businesses, and organizations thrive in an increasingly digital world.

We specialize in custom website development, mobile applications, enterprise software, e-commerce platforms, and API integrations—solutions built to solve real business challenges, not just to “exist online.” Our approach is client-focused, ensuring that every project we execute is tailored to meet the unique goals and aspirations of those we serve.
