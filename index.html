What makes MIDCRIB different from other IT companies?

At MIDCRIB, we don’t just build digital products—we craft tailored solutions that align with your business goals. Our approach is client-first, ensuring every project is delivered with innovation, precision, and measurable results. With experience across startups, NGOs, enterprises, and government agencies, we bring both versatility and industry expertise to the table.

How long does it take to develop a custom website?

The timeline depends on the complexity of the project. A simple business website may take 2–4 weeks, while a complex enterprise-level platform may require 8–12 weeks or more. We always provide a detailed project timeline after understanding your requirements.

Do you offer mobile app development for both Android and iOS?

Yes, we develop native apps (Android & iOS), cross-platform apps using frameworks like Flutter and React Native, and progressive web apps (PWAs). We ensure performance, security, and scalability across all platforms.

Can you integrate third-party services into my website or app?

Absolutely. Our API Integration & Automation services enable seamless connection of your system with payment gateways, CRMs, ERPs, shipping providers, analytics platforms, and more—helping you save time and improve efficiency.

Do you provide e-commerce solutions with payment integration?

Yes, we develop scalable e-commerce platforms with secure payment gateways, inventory management, product catalogs, customer dashboards, and advanced analytics. Whether you’re a small business or a large retailer, we tailor solutions to fit your growth needs.

How do you ensure the security of my project?

Security is at the core of our development process. We implement best practices in encryption, authentication, secure coding, penetration testing, and regular security audits to ensure your platform is fully protected against cyber threats.
